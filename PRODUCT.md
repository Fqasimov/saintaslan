# Product

## Platform

web (Vite + React + TypeScript, static build). Intro is a Remotion composition played with `@remotion/player`; scroll-linked motion uses `motion`; smooth scrolling uses Lenis on desktop only.

## Users

Women in Azerbaijan, the Gulf and the diaspora who find Saint Aslan on Instagram, open the site on a phone, and want to know two things: what the pieces look like up close, and whether the piece is on the rail in the boutique near them.

## Product Purpose

Show the two seasons and turn interest into a reservation over WhatsApp with the right boutique. Success is a visitor sending a "Reserve in Baku / Dubai" message.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Couture with two boutiques: Baku and Dubai, each with its own stock. Visitors choose a boutique; the choice is remembered and drives every WhatsApp link.
- Two seasons: Winter (velvet) and Summer 2026 (chiffon, satin, a polka dot).
- No cart, no prices, no sizes on the site.
- English only.
- Unknown and not to be invented: addresses, phone numbers, prices, sizes, fabric composition, founding year, press, clients.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Saint Aslan. On the campaign cover the name is set in wide geometric capitals in taupe, with "SUMMER 2026" small in oxblood.
- Product cards caption pieces in a Didone italic ("Velvet Waist-fitted Zip Corset").
- "Aslan" is the Azerbaijani word for lion.
- The logo file is still to come; the wordmark component is the placeholder.

## Product Principles

1. The cloth leads: velvet is shown up close and drawn before it is shown.
2. Every piece is one tap from the right boutique on WhatsApp.
3. The two cities are equals: same hour, same weight on the page.
4. Never claim what the house has not confirmed.
